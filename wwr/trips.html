Our Trips

Half-Day Rapids Adventure

A thrilling experience with light to moderate rapids, ideal for those seeking an adventurous yet manageable ride. An action-packed journey through exciting rapids and breathtaking scenery, designed to deliver adventure without overwhelming rafters.

Full-Day Rafting Experience

Spend the day on the river, navigating multiple rapids with breaks for swimming and a riverside lunch. This full-day adventure offers a perfect blend of thrilling rapids and peaceful river stretches, providing an unforgettable outdoor experience for both beginners and seasoned rafters.

Raft and Zipline Combo

Combine a half-day rafting adventure with a zipline tour for a full day of fun and excitement. Soar through the treetops on a thrilling zipline course after conquering exhilarating rapids, making it the ultimate combo for adrenaline junkies and nature lovers alike.
